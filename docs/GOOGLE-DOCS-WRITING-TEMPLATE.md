## Final pre-publication questions

- Am I ready for people I know to read this?
- Is anyone identifiable who has not chosen to be part of this story?
- Would changing a name, date, location, or detail protect someone without changing my truth?
- Am I publishing because I choose to, rather than because I feel pressured to explain?
- What response, if any, am I willing to receive after publication?
- Do I want to wait forty-eight hours and read it again?
