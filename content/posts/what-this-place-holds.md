There are things I have carried quietly for a long time. Some are heavy: grief, illness, the difficult negotiations of living in a body with limits, and the parts of mental health that are hard to explain in ordinary conversation. Some are gentle: an act of care, a remembered voice, an ordinary moment that became precious because it could not last.

I wanted a place capable of holding both.

This archive is where I can tell stories when I am ready, in the shape that feels honest. They may arrive as essays, letters, fragments, or small notes. Some may have a clear ending. Others may stop exactly where my understanding stops.

## For the people who know me

Some of these stories will be new, even to people I know and love. That does not mean I was hiding a finished explanation. Often I was still living through the experience, trying to find language for it, or protecting something that did not yet feel ready to be seen.

Publishing a page means I am ready to share that page. It does not mean I am ready to answer every question around it.

## For anyone carrying something similar

I hope there can be recognition here without comparison. Pain does not need to compete for legitimacy. A limited body is still a whole life. Grief can contain love, anger, numbness, gratitude, and contradiction at the same time.

Nothing here needs to become a lesson before it deserves a place.

This is not a complete account of who I am. It is a shelf for what I choose to set down: memories both good and bad, difficult truths, and little notes of love and care. You may begin anywhere. You may leave at any time. The pages will remain.
