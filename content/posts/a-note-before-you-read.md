Some pages in this archive hold grief, mental health struggles, illness, hospitals, or the realities of living inside physical limits. When a piece contains material that may be difficult to encounter unexpectedly, it begins with a plain content note.

That note is an invitation to choose, not a warning that you must prove you can continue.

You are allowed to stop. You are allowed to save a page for another day. You are allowed to read only the gentler pieces. Care for the reader belongs here too.

## What sharing means

These are personal experiences, written from one point of view. A piece may capture what something felt like at a particular moment without becoming the only possible account of what happened.

Sharing a story does not mean I need it solved. It does not automatically ask for advice, reassurance, comparison, or an explanation of what I should have done differently. Sometimes the kindest response to a difficult truth is simply to let it be heard.

It also does not make every adjacent part of my life public. There may be names I change, details I leave out, or stories I hold back entirely. Those boundaries are part of the truth too.

## What this place can offer

This website is not medical advice or crisis support. It is a personal archive: one person making room for experiences that have been difficult to carry and difficult to say.

If something here helps you feel less alone, I am glad. If a page is not what you need, you can close it without apology.

Read slowly. Keep your own boundaries. Let mine remain mine.
